It notices the country you are in Pro

Phileas marks each day with the country you spent it in, and a day in two countries keeps both. It does this once you allow location Always: at first it asks only for location while you use the app, and offers Always afterwards.

The limits you have to keep, counted every day Pro

Schengen’s 90 days in 180. The 183 days of a tax residency. A limit like that is easy to break by miscounting, and costly when you do.

Phileas counts it for you. Add Schengen or 183 days ready-made, or make a rule of your own — in a country or away from it, at most or at least — and see the days you have used, the days left or still needed, and the last day if you stay.

A summer across Europe, a residency you have to keep up, a country you would rather not stay in too long: one rule each, one number each, so you know where you stand before you book the next flight.

Informational only — not legal or tax advice. These rules have exceptions this app does not model. Check with a professional before acting on a number here.

When it doesn’t know, it says so

A day Phileas has no country for remains unknown, and is shown as unknown: it is never filled in silently. Phileas asks you about the days it is missing, and you answer by saying where you were.

Your trip around the world, on a globe

Every country you have been to, on a globe you can turn, coloured by the days you spent in each: this year, or across all your history. Two figures say how far you have been: the places you have visited and the share of the map they make up.

The Map and Places widgets put your places and the two figures on your Home Screen. The map and the widgets are free.

The map is drawn on your phone from data that comes with the app. It downloads nothing.

Every country counted

Every country you have been to, ranked by its days and its share of the period: this month, this year, the last 180 days, the last 12 months or dates you choose. Touch one to see its days month by month and your trips there.

Your rule with its number on your Home Screen, or your countries. A rule’s widget colours its pace as its page does, and the medium Countries widget shows your top ten. A tap opens what the widget shows.

Free and Pro

Free

By hand is free, always: you say where you were — a stay of several days or one day — and Phileas counts the days, on the calendar, the ranking and the map, with the map widgets. Exporting your data, or a PDF report of your days, is free too.

Pro

Automatic is Pro: Phileas notices the country once you allow location Always, and adds rules, the Rule and Countries widgets, photos and another app’s file. It starts with 14 days free, and nothing is charged when they end.

Questions

Does my data leave my phone?

Nothing leaves your phone unless you export it and send it somewhere yourself. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. If you buy Pro, Apple handles the purchase. Your phone’s own backup — iCloud or a computer — includes this app’s data, as it does for most apps. The app does not send it there; your phone does, if backups are on.

Can I use it just to see where I’ve been?

Yes, and for free: say where you have been, a stay of several days or one day, and Phileas draws your countries on the globe and counts the places you have visited, with the map widgets on your Home Screen. Reading your photos to fill in the past, and noticing the country by itself, are Pro.

What happens when it doesn’t know where I am?

The day stays unknown, and Phileas shows it as unknown: it never fills it in silently. It asks you about the days it is missing, and you answer by saying where you were, for one day or for a stay of several.

Do I need internet?

Phileas counts your days on your phone. The map is drawn on your phone from data that comes with the app. It downloads nothing. Buying Pro, starting the 14-day trial or restoring a purchase goes through the App Store, which needs a connection.

Does Andorra count toward the Schengen area?

Only if you turn it on. Monaco, San Marino, Vatican City and Andorra count as their own countries, and toward the Schengen area only if you turn them on, in Settings under Territories. Phileas counts days in Åland as days in the Schengen area. Phileas counts days in Gibraltar as days in the Schengen area from 15 July 2026, as the UK government says.
