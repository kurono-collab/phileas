Soporte

English

Phileas counts the days you spend in each country, so you can keep to visa and tax-residency limits such as Schengen’s 90 days in 180. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no server, no analytics. If you buy Pro, Apple handles the purchase.

Questions, problems or a day counted wrong: write to support@phileasfogg.app.

Español
